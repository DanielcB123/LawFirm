# Personal injury

[Home](../README.md) · [Matter core](../modules/matter-management.md) · [Settlement](../billing/trust-and-settlement.md)

Model incident facts, involved parties, insurance policies and coverage assertions, adjusters, liability evidence, treatment episodes, providers, medical-record requests, bills, claimed damages, wage loss, liens/subrogation interests, demands, offers, litigation, and settlement distributions.

Record whether a coverage amount or lien figure is reported, verified, disputed, or estimated. Preserve source documents and verification dates. Deduplicate medical requests without conflating records with bills. Sensitive medical information receives restricted access even within the firm.

Configurable phases: intake/conflicts, engagement, investigation, treatment/record collection, damages assessment, demand, negotiation, suit if appropriate, discovery, mediation/trial, settlement, lien resolution, distribution, closure. Do not treat treatment progress as legal case-completion progress.

Contingency agreements must version fee tiers and cost treatment with attorney approval. Settlement calculations show gross, agreement-based fees, reimbursable expenses, liens/third-party amounts, reserves, and net to the client. A client sees only the reviewed statement applicable to their entitlement.

HIPAA applicability is determined by role and relationship, not simply by possessing injury records. HHS explains covered-entity/business-associate relationships [S14]. Assess applicable agreements, subcontractor obligations, data safeguards, authorizations, and other confidentiality requirements before selecting medical-record/AI providers. Do not claim blanket HIPAA compliance.

Pilot acceptance covers a record request awaiting response, restricted medical uploads, a disputed lien, a changed settlement version invalidating approval, and an approved client status update. All legal dates and authority decisions remain reviewable by the attorney.
