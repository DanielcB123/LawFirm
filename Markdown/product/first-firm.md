# Maldanado and Benitez Law — first real tenant

This firm is the founding real customer identified by the founder, not a fictional seed-data example. Confirm its exact name, jurisdiction, legal entity, contacts, authorized decision makers, practice mix, and any investor relationship directly with the firm. Real tenant configuration does not authorize importing real client files into development.

## Discovery sessions
Walk through one construction dispute and one personal injury matter using sanitized examples. Map every current tool, manual handoff, duplicate entry, recurring client question, deadline source, fee arrangement, and closure requirement. Interview an attorney, paralegal, finance user, and consenting client representative where feasible.

Record mandatory forms, engagement templates, conflict procedures, joint-client policies, signature needs, bank/trust arrangements, existing data exports, communications preferences, accessibility needs, and staff approval responsibilities. Confirm whether Spanish support is required rather than assuming it from the firm's name.

## Rollout
Provision a genuine firm tenant in staging with synthetic cases. Validate role assignments, domain ownership, billing settings, templates, published-content policy, and restoration. Agree on a limited production cohort and workflow ownership. Run parallel deadline and financial checks during adoption. Import approved data through a repeatable migration with reconciliation and exception reports. Never fabricate investment terms or silently enroll actual clients.

## First-firm acceptance record
Track requirement, owner, demo evidence, exception, decision date, and sign-off. Commercial readiness, legal workflow readiness, and technical readiness are separate. First-customer success should lead to reusable configuration; avoid a fork of the product uniquely maintained for this firm.
