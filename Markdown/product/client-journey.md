# Consultation to closure

[Home](../README.md) · [Intake](../modules/intake-and-consultations.md) · [Portal](../modules/client-portal.md)

| Stage | Client experience | Staff work and gate |
|---|---|---|
| Inquiry | Minimal contact and matter category | Capture source, urgency, limited conflict information |
| Consultation | Available time, confirmation, reschedule | Availability, time zone, conflict triage |
| Intake | Save progress, structured forms, safe uploads | Review completeness and conflicts |
| Engagement | Review terms, sign, make applicable payment | Attorney acceptance and executed agreement |
| Matter opening | Welcome, team, next steps | Assign owner, verified deadlines, practice template |
| Active work | Approved timeline, requests, appointments, messages | Tasks, evidence, internal work, published updates |
| Resolution | Review approved offer or settlement documents | Attorney checks authority, fees, liens, obligations |
| Distribution/billing | Approved financial statement and receipts | Cleared funds, reconciliation, authorized disbursement |
| Closure | Closing letter, permitted export, access instructions | Retention, outstanding work, legal hold, final account |

Model inquiry, engagement, matter phase, financial state, and archival state separately. One status field cannot represent all of them. Matter phases vary by practice and may loop or reopen.

## Example workflow
A construction client books a consultation, passes conflicts review, signs an engagement, and receives a matter workspace. Staff request the contract and change orders. The client uploads them; files remain quarantined until scanning completes. The attorney publishes an approved update explaining the next step. The portal shows that update and the client's outstanding request, not the attorney's internal negotiation notes.

## Acceptance
One end-to-end trial must cover booking, denied conflict review, successful engagement, matter assignment, safe document upload, published update, message, applicable payment, resolution, and authorized export. Include cancellation, no-show, rejected payment, representative revocation, and reopened matter paths. A declined prospective client retains appropriate confidentiality protections and does not receive unrelated matter access.
