# Mission, outcomes, and scope

[Home](../README.md) · [Client journey](client-journey.md) · [Roadmap](../delivery/roadmap-and-acceptance.md)

## Outcomes
Clients can answer: What is happening? What is needed from me? What happens next? Who is responsible? What have I paid or owe? Staff can answer: Which matters are blocked, overdue, under-updated, or financially unreconciled?

The system should reduce routine status inquiries, shorten intake turnaround, prevent missed work, and make approved information easy to find. Measure inquiries per active matter, time from inquiry to consultation, intake completion, overdue tasks, update freshness, and successful client task completion. Set baseline values with the first firm before claiming improvement.

## Users and responsibilities

| User | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| Prospective client | Minimal inquiry, booking, intake submission |
| Represented client | Assigned matters, approved documents, messages, payments |
| Authorized representative | Explicit, scoped, revocable access; never inferred from family relationship |
| Attorney | Conflicts decisions, legal decisions, publication, deadline verification |
| Paralegal/staff | Assigned matter work and client support within permissions |
| Firm administrator | Membership, configuration, operational reporting |
| Finance role | Billing, reconciliation, permitted financial reporting |
| Platform operator | Platform operation; no default access to matter contents |
| Investor | Commercial information under agreement; no case access by virtue of investing |

## Scope boundaries
Include consultation scheduling, intake, conflicts workflow, engagement, matter work, secure portal, documents, evidence, calendar, deadlines, billing, payment reconciliation, settlement workflow, reporting, and controlled integrations.

Stage advanced research, AI drafting, e-filing, complex discovery review, payroll, and full general accounting behind evidence of need. Build cohesive matter workflows first. An integrated provider is acceptable where it avoids reinventing regulated or licensed infrastructure.

Do not invent the firm's staff, addresses, fee arrangements, court jurisdictions, contracts, investment terms, or existing software. Do not imply that submission, booking, or account creation automatically establishes representation. Attorney-approved wording and process must reflect actual practice.
